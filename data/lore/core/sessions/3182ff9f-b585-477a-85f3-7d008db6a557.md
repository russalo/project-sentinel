I’ll pay what I can for help." Nearby, a Hollowbrook villager, Garek, clutches a rusted key. "The beast came for the mayor’s daughter last night," he rasps. "It’s guarding something in the temple. We think... the amulet cursed our land." His gaze drops to the key—a relic that hums with residual shadow-magic.

A low growl rumbles through the fog. The shadows between the trees thicken, coalescing into a pair of glowing red eyes. Thalia nocks an arrow. "Choose, Shadowmancer. Fight with me or let them take their prey first."
